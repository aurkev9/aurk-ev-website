AUR-K EV website starter
========================

Included:
- Responsive premium-style showroom landing page
- Scooter catalogue with category filters
- Shopping bag/cart interaction
- WhatsApp enquiry checkout flow (requires your number)
- About, benefits and contact sections

Important before publishing:
1. WhatsApp is configured for AUR-K EV: +92 323 9661747.
2. Confirm your scooter range, descriptions, photos and prices in the PRODUCTS array. Current catalogue includes ROADKING R99 PRO and ROADKING RAPTOR as enquiry-only items.
3. Showroom phone and address have been added to index.html.
4. Replace generic scooter photography with your own AUR-K EV product photos/posters.
5. This is a front-end starter, not a production payment/store backend. It does not process card payments, maintain stock, calculate delivery, or save orders to a database.
6. Domain aurkev.com must be registered and connected to a hosting provider separately.

To preview: open index.html in a modern browser.


Launch payment setup:
- Cash on delivery is the only launch payment option.
- Cart checkout opens WhatsApp with the selected scooter models and asks the customer to provide name, delivery address and contact number.
- Confirm stock, final price, delivery coverage and delivery charges with the customer before accepting an order.
- JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer and card gateways are deferred until a later phase.
- This starter does not store orders in a backend or process online payments.
